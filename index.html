Program highlights

A degree that builds both circuits and code

Computer Engineering combines the practical work of electrical engineering with the problem-solving approach of computer science. The program brings together laboratory work, hardware fundamentals, and software development.

  • Hardware and software foundation: digital logic, circuits, microprocessors, and programming in one curriculum.
  • Project-based learning: design projects that bring embedded systems, networks, and software together.
  • Community and mentorship: SCPES workshops, competitions, and peer support.
  • Versatile careers: opportunities in embedded systems, networking, AI, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity.
