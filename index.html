Program highlights

A degree that builds both circuits and code

Computer Engineering sits at the intersection of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. At UE, that means hands-on labs plus rigorous software training.

  • Hardware + software foundation — digital logic, circuits, microprocessors, and programming in one curriculum.
  • Project-based learning — design projects that integrate embedded systems, networks, and software.
  • Community & mentorship — SCPES workshops, competitions, and peer support.
  • Versatile careers — from embedded and network engineering to AI, cloud, and cybersecurity.
