Academic support

Beyond the classroom

CpE faculty at UE engage students through multiple channels beyond lectures:

  • Capstone advising — faculty mentor year-long design projects from proposal to defense.
  • Research collaboration — undergraduate research assistantships, conference travel, and publication support.
  • Competition coaching — SCPES-organized hackathons, robotics contests, and programming competitions.
  • Industry linkages — faculty maintain ties with semiconductor, telco, and software firms for internships and OJT.
  • Licensure review — dedicated review classes for the Computer Engineering board exam.
