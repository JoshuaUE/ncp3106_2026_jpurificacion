Who we are

SCPES at a glance

The Student Council of Computer Engineering Students (SCPES) is the official student organization representing BS Computer Engineering majors at the University of the East. We bridge students, faculty, and industry — building community, skills, and opportunities.

Our objectives
  • Academic excellence — peer tutoring, review sessions, and study groups for core CpE courses.
  • Technical skill-building — workshops, hackathons, and certification prep beyond the curriculum.
  • Industry readiness — tech talks, company visits, resume clinics, and internship matching.
  • Community & welfare — social events, mental health awareness, and student advocacy.
  • Leadership development — officer training, project management, and public speaking opportunities.
  • Alumni engagement — mentorship programs, career panels, and networking events.

Activities

What we do — a typical year

Week-long celebration with technical competitions, exhibit of student projects, alumni homecoming, and the CpE Night social.

February Competition Showcase

24-hour hardware+software hackathon. Themes rotate: IoT, robotics, AI/edge, fintech. Industry judges, prize pools, hiring pipelines.

July Hackathon Prizes

Monthly hands-on sessions: PCB design (KiCad), FPGA (Verilov), ROS 2, TensorRT, Kubernetes, Git/GitHub, LaTeX for thesis. Free for members.

Monthly Hands-on Certificates

Engineers from semiconductor, telco, software, and startup firms share real projects. Site visits to fabs, data centers, and R&D labs.

Quarterly Networking Internships

Represent UE in: ICPC programming, robotics sumo/line-follow, IoT challenge, cybersecurity CTF, chip design contests.

Year-round Travel Awards

Kumustahan sessions, mental health webinars, buddy system for freshmen, academic counseling referrals, exam care packages.
